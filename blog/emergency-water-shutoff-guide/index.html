Finding Your Water Shutoff Valve in Sioux City, IA

Home / Blog / How to Shut Off Your Water in an Emergency in Sioux City, IA

Stopping the flow of water is the quickest way to limit damage during a water emergency. Knowing how to locate and operate your water shutoff valve could save you thousands of dollars and prevent extensive property damage. Every Sioux City, IA homeowner should know their shutoff valve location and how to operate it before an emergency occurs.

Locating Your Main Water Shutoff Valve

In most Sioux City homes, the main water shutoff valve is located in one of these areas: near the front of the house where the water line enters from the street, in the garage along an interior wall, in the basement or crawl space near the foundation wall, in a utility room or laundry area, or in a ground-level access box near the street (the meter shutoff). Two types are typical: gate valves, which have a round handle, and ball valves, which have a lever. A quarter turn shuts off a ball valve, and that style is also more reliable.

How to Shut Off Water Quickly

A gate valve closes when you turn its handle clockwise, to the right, for several full turns, to the point where it will not go further. For ball valves, turn the lever handle perpendicular to the pipe. A valve that will not move should not be forced, as breaking it would make things worse. In that case, shut off water at the meter using a meter key or adjustable wrench.

Individual Fixture Shutoffs

You can usually stop water to one fixture or appliance without cutting off the whole house, because most have a shutoff valve of their own. Look for shutoff valves under sinks, behind toilets, behind washing machines, and near water heaters. Learn where each one is so you can quickly stop a leak in one place and still have water everywhere else in the house.
