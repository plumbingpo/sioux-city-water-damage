Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. For flood emergencies anywhere in Sioux City, Sioux City Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
